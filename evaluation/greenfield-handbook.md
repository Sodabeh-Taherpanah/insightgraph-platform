# Greenfield Logistics GmbH: Employee Handbook (Test Document)

This is a fictional handbook written for testing a document question-answering system. All names, numbers and policies are invented.

## 1. Company overview

Greenfield Logistics GmbH was founded in 2011 in Hamburg by Marlene Voss and Tobias Brandt. The company moves temperature-sensitive goods, mainly vaccines, fresh fish and flowers, between warehouses in Germany, Poland and the Netherlands. In 2025 the company employed 412 people and operated 87 refrigerated trucks. The headquarters is on Elbstrasse 14 in Hamburg. The largest warehouse is in Poznan and has 18,000 square metres of cold storage.

## 2. Working hours and remote work

The standard working week is 38 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when everyone should be reachable, are 10:00 to 15:00. Office staff may work from home up to three days per week after the probation period. Drivers and warehouse staff follow shift plans that are published every second Thursday for the following two weeks. Overtime must be approved by the team lead in advance and is compensated with time off at a rate of 1.25 hours per overtime hour. Night shifts, between 22:00 and 06:00, receive a supplement of 18 percent.

## 3. Leave and holidays

Every full-time employee receives 30 days of paid annual leave. Leave requests longer than 10 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 8 weeks in advance. Unused leave can be carried over until 31 March of the following year, but not longer. Sick leave is paid in full for the first 6 weeks. From the first day of absence, a medical certificate is required after 3 calendar days. Parental leave follows the legal rules, and the company adds a one-time birth bonus of 500 euros per child.

## 4. Onboarding and probation

New employees have a probation period of 6 months. During the first week, every new hire completes the safety training and meets a buddy, who is an experienced colleague from the same team. After 30, 60 and 90 days there is a short feedback talk with the team lead. Laptops are handed out on the first day, and the IT helpdesk can be reached at extension 4400 or through the ticket portal. Warehouse and driving roles also complete a cold-chain course lasting 2 days.

## 5. Cold-chain rules

The cold chain must never be interrupted. Vaccines are transported between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Fresh fish is transported at 0 to 2 degrees Celsius. Flowers are transported between 4 and 8 degrees Celsius. Every truck carries two independent temperature loggers that record a value every 5 minutes. If the temperature leaves the allowed range for more than 15 minutes, the driver must call the control centre immediately and the shipment is marked as "at risk". The control centre is staffed 24 hours a day and its emergency number is 0800 555 0199. Logger data is stored for 5 years for audit purposes.

## 6. Travel and expenses

Business travel must be booked through the internal travel portal. Train travel in second class is the default for distances under 600 kilometres. Flights are allowed above that distance or when the train takes more than 7 hours. The hotel limit is 130 euros per night in Germany and 150 euros per night in other countries. Meals are reimbursed with a flat daily allowance of 28 euros for a full travel day. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after the trip, otherwise they are not reimbursed. Receipts above 25 euros are required.

## 7. Learning and development

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,200 euros and 5 paid learning days. Courses are approved by the team lead. The company pays for language courses in German, Polish and English if they are relevant for the role. If an employee leaves within 12 months after a course that cost more than 500 euros, a proportional part of the cost must be repaid. A yearly development talk takes place every November.

## 8. IT security and data protection

Passwords must have at least 14 characters and are changed only when a compromise is suspected. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Company laptops use full-disk encryption. Customer data, such as delivery addresses and patient-related shipment information, may only be stored in approved systems and never in personal cloud storage. Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 2 hours. The data protection officer is Ingrid Falk, and she can be contacted at datenschutz@greenfield-logistics.example. Personal data of customers is deleted 24 months after the last shipment unless a legal obligation requires longer storage.

## 9. Sustainability goals

Greenfield Logistics plans to reduce its CO2 emissions per transported tonne by 40 percent by 2030, compared with 2019. By the end of 2027, 30 of the 87 refrigerated trucks should be fully electric. The Poznan warehouse already covers 55 percent of its energy demand with rooftop solar panels. Employees who commute by bicycle receive a bonus of 15 euros per month.
